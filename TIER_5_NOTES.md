# X BOT Tier 5 — Guided Nation Progress

Tier 5 makes the existing game easier to learn and faster to continue without adding more public slash commands.

## Player progression

- The Lobby now shows Nation Level, Nation rank, XP progress, and one recommended next action.
- **Missions** is a multi-page panel for Starter, Daily, and Weekly objectives.
- Mission progress is calculated from existing Economy, Mining, City, Army, Land, and Battle records.
- Existing players receive credit for earlier permanent achievements such as Cities, recruited forces, mining, and claimed Land.
- **Claim Ready** collects every completed reward on the current page in one press.
- Every reward claim has a unique database key and cannot be redeemed twice after a restart.
- Nation XP raises a Nation through ten ranks. Level-up rewards are granted automatically.
- Progress ranks guide the player without locking old gameplay features.

## Faster play

- **Continue** in the Lobby and Missions goes directly to the system needed for the next objective.
- Existing quantity controls remain the fast path for Shop purchases and Army recruitment.
- Existing Sell Mining Materials, City Build Several, multi-city Upgrade, and Upgrade Every City remain connected to their main panels.
- Mission results edit the same Discord message instead of creating extra channel output.

## Reliability and staff tools

- Tier 5 uses additive tables, so old databases migrate without removing or rewriting player records.
- `/admin` System Status now reports Tier 4 and Tier 5 health together.
- `/admin` includes **Repair Missions**, which creates missing profiles and normalises invalid Nation progress safely.
- The existing Tester Feedback panel remains the one-click route for bugs and suggestions.

## Tier 6 target — Economy

Tier 6 will focus on the Economy: rebalance income and prices, improve item value and sinks, add clearer market history, make jobs/business activity worthwhile, and provide administrators with an Economy health and balancing dashboard.
