# X BOT New Player Guide

X BOT is designed around panels. You only need a few short slash commands; most actions are buttons and menus inside the same Discord message.

## 1. Start here

1. Use `/lobby` to open the main centre.
2. Claim `/daily` for 50 XC.
3. Open `/economy` to see your Wallet, Bank, Backpack, Mining, Market, Stock Market, Contracts and Production.
4. Open `/war` to see your Nation, Cities, Armed Forces, Diplomacy and Warfront.

Every major result has a button back to its centre or the Lobby. Pressing a button edits the current panel whenever possible, so the channel is not filled with bot messages.

## 2. Build your economy

- `/mine` is the fastest way to gather materials. The result panel lets you mine again, sell materials, or return to Economy.
- `/economy` opens the complete Economy Centre. Use its pages for Mining, Shop, Backpack, Market, Bank, Contracts, Production and Stocks.
- `/sell_item` is a fast shortcut when you already know what you want to sell.
- `/collect` collects your 12-hour City production when it is ready.
- `/craft` opens the Crafting Centre. Recipes and production costs are shown before confirmation.
- `/stock` opens the virtual Stock Market. Prices can rise or fall; never invest XC that you still need for normal gameplay.

Recommended first loop: **Daily → Mine → Sell → Contracts → Build/upgrade Cities → Collect**.

## 3. Develop your Nation

Open `/war`, then use **City**.

- **Build** lets you choose one or several available Lands and build Cities in one transaction.
- **Upgrade** lets you select one or several Cities and upgrade them together.
- **Land** shows real territories and their City capacity.
- **Collect** pays the production from your Capital and Cities every 12 hours.
- City and Nation rename tools are available inside their panels; you do not need to remember extra commands.

Use `/map` for the world view and `/map_detail` for one Nation's real Land. `/claim_land` claims one connected free province/state when its cooldown is ready.

## 4. Build an army

Use `/recruit` for the fastest access, or open `/war` → **Army**.

1. Recruit at least one Land Army model. An attack cannot launch without Land units.
2. Buy Supply in the Army page.
3. Use **Prepare** to restore Readiness and **Rally** to restore Morale.
4. Air and Navy units support the Land battle. Different unit models and tanks contribute their configured Power.

## 5. Fight with Warfront 2.0

First use `/diplomacy` or `/declare_war` against a bordering Nation. Then use `/attack` or `/war` → **Attack**.

The Attack Planner has five quick steps:

1. Choose an enemy Nation.
2. Choose a front-line Land.
3. Select one or many unit models.
4. Choose a mode: **Recon Probe**, **Standard Assault**, or **Breakthrough**.
5. Press **Preview**, then **Launch Attack**.

Large Nations and armies show **Next Land** or **Next Units** buttons, so every choice remains available even after the first 25 entries.

The preview shows estimated win chance, power ranges, terrain and fortification before anything is spent. **Quick Attack** automatically chooses an active enemy, the weakest valid front-line Land and a Standard Assault.

Ordinary Land must be captured before a Capital can be attacked. Battle results include Air, Navy and Land fronts, losses, captured Land or Capital damage. Use **All Reports** to reopen permanent battle details.

## 6. Defend your Nation

Open `/war` → **Defence**.

- Choose a Land and press **Fortify Selected**.
- **Fortify Weak Lands** upgrades several weakest Lands in one click when you have enough War Credits.
- Garrison commitment controls how much of your army automatically joins a defence.
- **Capital Priority** adds extra Capital protection.
- **Auto Reinforce** spends Supply to improve low Readiness before an incoming battle.
- Terrain, Cities, fortifications and your defence stance all affect defensive power.

## Fast command list

Main centres: `/lobby`, `/economy`, `/war`, `/casino`, `/craft`, `/city`, `/army`, `/recruit`, `/diplomacy`, `/mining`, `/stock`.

Fast actions: `/daily`, `/collect`, `/mine`, `/sell_item`, `/map`, `/map_detail`, `/claim_land`, `/declare_war`, `/attack`, `/balance`, `/code_redeem`.

If a panel becomes old or Discord says the interaction expired, open the centre command again. Confirm buttons are protected against duplicate clicks.
