# X BOT Beta 1.4B — Verification & Applications

- `/verification_panel` posts a persistent verification button.
- Verification gives the configured Guest role.
- Reaching Level 2 automatically removes Guest and gives the permanent Member reward role.
- Optional minimum Discord account age and verification log channel.
- `/apply` opens an in-Discord application form with up to five editable questions.
- `/application_review` lets configured reviewers accept, deny, or hold a submission.
- Accepted applications can automatically grant a configured Discord role.
- Review and result channels are selected by channel name in the Dashboard.
- Dashboard controls forms, questions, reviewer roles, accepted roles, cooldowns, statuses, and recent submissions.
- Level reward duplicate seeding is fixed; deleted duplicate rows do not return after restart.
- Level announcement, Job log, and Log Settings use server channel dropdowns.
- Pre-upgrade backup: `backups/xwar-before-beta-1.4B.db`.
